Pacific Rhododendron

Rhododendron macrophyllum

The State Flower of Washington

Description

The pacific rhododendron (also called the California rose bay, California rhododendron, coast rhododendron or big leaf rhododendron) is a modest-sized flowering shrub than ranges from 2 to 9 meters tall. Its leaves can extend 3 to 9 inches long and 1 to 3 inches wide.

It is an evergreen, meaning that it retains its leaves during the fall and winter seasons. Its flowers are typically pink, but other morphs do exist.

Geography

This plant is found exclusively in the Pacfic Northwest along the mountainous regions and along the coastline. Its range extends as far south as Monterey Bay and as far north as British Columbia.
