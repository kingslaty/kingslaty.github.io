Ending #6: Home, Sweet Home...

"Oh yeah, sure thing! Uhh... where exactly do you live?" After a few agonizing minutes of attempting to explain what an address is, the monster taps a stick a few times and you find yourself wisked away, right in front of your house. You promptly lay down on the ground, releived to be home. "Is this the right place?" A thundering voice above you asks. The monster is clinging on your roof. But it's ok, you've learned he's pretty chill now. Thumbs up, you're good. You two should meet for lunch some time.
