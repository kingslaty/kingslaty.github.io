Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

A delicatable treat mixing peanut butter and chocolate with cookies, this is a combination sure to delight.

Ingredients

  • 2½ cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 stick unsalted butter
  • ¾ peanut butter
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs + 1 egg yolk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350ºF.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
  3. heat butter in a small pot within a similar sized pot containing hot water.
  4. Once butter is melted, add peanut butter, sugar, and brown sugar. Stir until well mixed.
  5. Add the eggs, yolk, and vanilla one at a time, until fully mixed.
  6. Mix the flour and in with dry ingridients. Once done, add the liquid mixture and chocolate chips slowly.
  7. Roll dough into equal sized balls and place onto baking sheets. Bake for 12 minutes, or until golden brown.
  8. Cool once done, and enjoy.
