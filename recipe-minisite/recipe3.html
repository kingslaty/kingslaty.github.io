Spagetti Aglio e Olio

A simple, flavorful, easy to prepare meal using a minimal ammount of ingridients.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped mushrooms
  • ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 4 teaspoons garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 pound spagetti
  • parmesan cheese (optional)

Preparation

  1. Heat the olive oil on the stove in a small saucepan on medium-low heat until simmering.
  2. Add the mushrooms to the oil and heat until mushrooms are tender.
  3. Remove from heat and add 3 teaspoons of garlic powder and mix.
  4. Boil water in a medium pot and add spagetti, stirring with a fork to seperate.
  5. Drain water from pot, saving 1¼ of the pasta water.
  6. Mix ¼ of the pasta water and remaining teaspoon of garlic powder in a seperate bowl.
  7. Add remaining pasta water and pasta to a large sauce pan, along with mushroom-garlic olive oil and salt. Stir well.
  8. Bring to boil and stir constantly, until most of the liquid is absorbed.
  9. Remove from heat and let stand for 5 minutes.
  10. Add remaining pasta water and red pepper flakes, toss to mix.
  11. Serve immediately, adding parmesan if desired.
